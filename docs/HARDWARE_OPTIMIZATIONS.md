## 2. Processor-by-Processor Optimization Breakdown

### 2.1. AMD Zen 5 (`Ryzen 9 9955HX / 9950X`, `Ryzen 7 9800X3D`, `EPYC 9005 Turin`)
1. **Single-Cycle 512-Bit Datapath (`_mm512_rorv_epi64`, `_mm512_permutex2var_epi8`, `VPDPBUSD`, & 8-`zmm` GDN)**:
   - Unlike Zen 4 (which double-pumps 512-bit instructions across 256-bit execution units), Zen 5 executes 512-bit AVX-512 instructions in a single clock cycle.
   - In `ggml_vec_dot_fractal_64_q8_0` (`ggml/src/ggml-cpu/quants.c`), `_mm512_rorv_epi64` (`vprorvq`) extracts the 64-bit upper bit-plane (`qh`) of `Fractal-64` (`GGML_TYPE_FRACTAL_64 = 44`) blocks in one instruction; dedicated 512-bit kernels also accelerate upstream `Q1_0 = 41` (`Qwen3.8` RaBitQ PLE), signed-scale `Q2_0 = 42`, and 36-layer multi-threaded `GATED_DELTA_NET`.
2. **48 KB L1D Cache 3D Tiling (`TILE_I0 = 64`) & `832 B` `RouteCrack 2.0 L1 Micro-Arbiter`**:
   - Zen 5 features a `48 KB` 12-way L1D cache per core (up from `32 KB` on Zen 4). Our 3D `MUL_MAT` tiler (`ggml-cpu.c`) tiles multi-head projections (`ne02 > 1`) in `64`-row chunks, while the `832 B` (`13` cache lines) `[10 -> 16 -> 1]` SIMD Micro-Arbiter (`common/rc_arbiter_l1.h`) remains 100% L1d-resident across decode steps.
3. **Victim-L3 Isolation (`_MM_HINT_NTA`) & `2MB` Transparent HugePages (`THP=madvise`)**:
   - Zen 5's `32 MB` per-CCD L3 cache is a victim cache filled by L2 evictions. Issuing `_mm_prefetch(..., _MM_HINT_NTA)` in `q4_0`, `mxfp4`, `fractal_64`, `vq3_64`, `q4_K`, and `q6_K` dot-product kernels protects the hot KV window (`LLAMA_KV_L3_WINDOW`) and `192 KB` `Stockfish-PVS` Zobrist tables. Inward `2 MiB`-aligned `MADV_HUGEPAGE` under `THP=madvise` expands L2 STLB coverage to `6.14 GB` without pre-faulting lazy `Qwen3.8` NVMe PLE pages.
4. **Built-In `LUT-VQ3-64` (`GGML_TYPE_VQ3_64 = 45`, `24 B / 64 W = 3.00 bpw`) via AVX-512 VBMI `VPERMI2B` ([`patches/llama-modchip-v3.2.patch`](../patches/llama-modchip-v3.2.patch))**:
   - **Zero-Collision GGML Tensor Type (`v3.0+`)**: Wired directly into `ggml` at slot `45` (leaving upstream `Q2_0 = 42` and `F8_E4M3 = 43` untouched) with multi-column GEMM/GEMV tiling (`_1x8`, `_1x4`, `_1x3`, `_1x2`, `1x1`) in `ggml/src/ggml-cpu/arch/x86/quants.c`, portable AVX2 + NEON + scalar fallbacks in `ggml/src/ggml-cpu/quants.c`, and Windows x64 PE/COFF unaligned `.rdata` loads (`_mm512_loadu_si512`).
   - **`block_vq3_64` Layout (`24 bytes` per `64` weights)**: `2B` `FP16` super-scale `d` and `22B` (`qs[22]`) packing `20` 7-bit 3D Lloyd-Max indices (`140 bits`, stride-22 weights), `2` 6-bit 2D tail indices (`12 bits`), and `24` high bits (`s24`) storing `8 × 3-bit` unsigned sub-scales (`mult = 2 + u_b ∈ {2..9}`).
   - **Register-Resident 3D Codebook Lookup**: All 128 3D `int8` centroids (`384 bytes` in `ggml/src/vq3_codebook.h`) stay in 6 ZMM registers (`cb0_lo/hi`, `cb1_lo/hi`, `cb2_lo/hi`), resolving 64 byte lookups per `_mm512_permutex2var_epi8` (`VPERMI2B`) instruction with zero L1/L2 cache reads and achieving **`13.57 dB` SQNR** on `BF16` MoE weights (`+3.42 dB` over `Q2_K`).

### 2.2. Multi-CCD AMD Threadripper PRO & EPYC (`7985WX` 8-CCD / `64C`)
1. **Cross-CCD Bandwidth Contention**:
   - On an 8-CCD processor (`64` cores sharing an 8-channel DDR5-6000 controller), launching 64 threads (`8` cores/CCD) causes atomic work-stealing contention and Infinity Fabric cache-coherency traffic, reducing decode throughput from `80.14 tok/s` to `51.80 tok/s`.
2. **Deterministic Per-CCD Stride (`4C/CCD` Sweet Spot)**:
   - [`scripts/llama_modchip.py`](../scripts/llama_modchip.py) queries `/sys/devices/system/cpu/cpu*/cache/index3/shared_cpu_list` to discover exact L3 CCD domains and selects `4` physical cores per CCD (`-t 32`), achieving **`386.00 tok/s` prefill**, **`80.14 tok/s` cold decode**, and **`111.62 tok/s` warm decode**.

### 2.3. Hybrid GPU + CPU MoE Systems (`Windows 10/11 CUDA`, `Linux/Windows AMD Vulkan`, & `Apple Silicon Metal`)
1. **Why Tensor-Role Splitting (`-ngl 99 --n-cpu-moe N --load-mode none`) Outperforms Layer-Index Splitting**:
   - Standard `-ngl N` offloads layers `0..N-1` (both attention/SSM and large MoE tensors) to GPU and leaves layers `N..L` entirely on CPU, forcing KV attention and dense projections on upper layers onto CPU RAM.
   - Tensor-Role Splitting (`-ngl 99 --n-cpu-moe N`) keeps **100% of the dense backbone, GQA/MLA KV cache, and Mamba-2 `SSM_SCAN` inside GPU VRAM (`616–1000 GB/s`)**, placing only $N$ sparse MoE expert layers (`ffn_*_exps`) in System RAM.
2. **Quantization Format Selection Across Backends**:
   - **Vulkan & CUDA GPU+CPU Hybrids (`Qwen3.8 MaxQ`, `DeepSeek-V4 MaxQ-v4`, `Nemotron MaxQ-16GB` / `Fractal-MXFP4` / `Q4_0`)**: 100% standard `ggml` formats (`<= 42`) execute natively on Vulkan and CUDA compute shaders (`75.1 / 80.8 tok/s` on `RX 9070 XT 16GB` with `Fractal-MXFP4`).
   - **Apple Silicon Metal (`Qwen3.8 MaxQ`, `DeepSeek-V4 MaxQ-v4`, `Nemotron MaxQ-16GB`, & `Fractal-64` `type 44`)**: [`patches/llama-modchip-v3.2.patch`](../patches/llama-modchip-v3.2.patch) integrates native register-tiled Metal shaders for `GGML_TYPE_FRACTAL_64 = 44` ([PR #24](https://github.com/synchopate/llama-modchip/pull/24)) alongside standard `ggml` `MaxQ` support (`78.6 tok/s` `Nemotron Fractal-64` on M3 Max; historical `27.0 tok/s` on 64 GB M5 Max was measured on the earlier withdrawn `DeepSeek-V4-Fractal` `56.56 GiB` build and awaits re-measurement on `MaxQ-v4`).
   - **x86-64 Pure CPU (`Qwen3.8 MaxQ-64GB/32GB`, `DS-V4 MaxQ-v4`, `Nemotron VQ3-64` `types 44/45`)**: Use `Nemotron VQ3-64` (`13.30 GiB`) on AVX-512 VBMI+VNNI CPUs (Zen 4/5, Ice Lake+) for lowest 16 GB RAM footprint, or `Nemotron MaxQ-16GB` / `Fractal-64` on AVX2-only CPUs.
3. **Windows WDDM Safety Margin (`windows/modchip_win.ps1`)**:
   - On Windows 10/11, exceeding physical VRAM causes the WDDM scheduler to page GPU allocations into shared system RAM (`GTT`) over PCIe. [`windows/modchip_win.ps1`](../windows/modchip_win.ps1) queries live free VRAM via `nvidia-smi` right before launch and reserves a `550 MB` WDDM safety margin.
